Bulk AI SEO Blog Refresh Engine

The idea

The idea is to apply a consistent refresh-review workflow across a larger set of article URLs.

The problem it addresses

Reviewing large content libraries page by page is slow and makes prioritization difficult.

Who it helps

Editors, content strategists, and writers improving existing or planned content.

What you can do

Crawls multiple URLs, evaluates refresh needs with AI, and organizes page-level SEO recommendations.

The intended outcome

Scale content-refresh analysis across many blog URLs.
